# v6.1 Accounting Acceptance Checklist

1. Login as Super Admin / Finance role.
2. Confirm Accounting sidebar shows Overview, Chart of Accounts, Journal Entries, General Ledger, Trial Balance, Financial Statements, GL Extraction, Opening Balances, Accounting Periods and Document Numbering.
3. Chart of Accounts: confirm expanded accounts and existing system accounts remain.
4. Journal Entries: confirm existing v6.0 journals are visible and totals balance.
5. General Ledger: select Cash and Bank; generate without dates, then with a date range; confirm running balance.
6. Trial Balance: confirm total debit equals total credit and closing debit equals closing credit.
7. Profit & Loss: confirm revenue, cost of sales, expenses and net result.
8. Balance Sheet: confirm Assets = Liabilities + Equity; investigate any displayed difference before sign-off.
9. Cash Flow: confirm opening cash + net movement = closing cash.
10. GL Extraction: search by journal number/reference and filter by account/date.
11. Export each report to Excel and PDF and confirm company name/report period are shown.
12. Opening Balances: test only in a controlled test period with a balanced journal.
13. Period lock: close a test period and confirm new postings into it are blocked.
14. Sync Operations to GL: run once, then run again; second run should skip already-linked operational entries.
