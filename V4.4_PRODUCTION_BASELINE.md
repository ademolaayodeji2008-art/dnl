# v4.4 Production Baseline

v4.4 is the clean-client distribution baseline.

Included from the tested application:
- Staff registration and strong-password security
- Forced temporary-password change
- Optional email verification
- Role-based access and Super Admin/Admin separation
- Configurable approval workflows
- Supporting documents and payment evidence
- Payment/receipt-confirmation lifecycle
- Reports, dashboards, Excel and print/PDF
- Audit Trail and System Health
- Backup/Restore Centre
- Company branding
- Responsive mobile sidebar/layout
- Clean Client Provisioning

Deployment/hosting infrastructure remains client/environment specific.
